## Implementation scope

Six destinations appear in Colorado Dreams and Funding. Tuition examples state period, residency, program, one-credit scope, fees, COF eligibility and pricing caveats. They are deliberately excluded from the existing semester-cost calculator. Fixed2027 dates never recur; annual patterns explicitly label the selected-year assumption. Unknown term/audience dates stay empty with an official-source prompt. The offline Zod importer validates supplied local JSON and citations; it does not fetch pages or change a student's plan.
